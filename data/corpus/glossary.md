# Glossary

- PSU: practical salinity unit, the unit used for salinity. Seawater is about 35 PSU.
- NTU: nephelometric turbidity unit, the unit used for turbidity.
- MPN: most probable number, a statistical estimate of bacterial concentration.
- Hypoxia: dissolved oxygen low enough to harm aquatic life; HEMP uses a threshold of 2 mg/L.
- Sonde: an instrument package that measures several water-quality variables at once.
