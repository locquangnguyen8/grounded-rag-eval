# Harvest Closure Rules

The regional food authority closes a harvest area when either trigger is met:

- more than 30 mm of rainfall falls in the catchment within 24 hours, or
- salinity at the harvest-area station stays below 22 PSU for two consecutive days.

A closed area can reopen only when both conditions hold: salinity has stayed above 22 PSU for three consecutive days, and a shellfish flesh sample tests below 230 E. coli MPN per 100 g.

HEMP data informs these decisions, but the food authority makes the final closure and reopening decision.
