# Dissolved Oxygen Alerts

Water with dissolved oxygen below 2 mg/L is treated as hypoxic. Hypoxia can kill shellfish and fish.

HEMP sends an alert when dissolved oxygen at any station stays below 2 mg/L for at least one hour. Alerts go to the program manager and to registered growers in the affected estuary.

Dissolved oxygen is usually lowest just before dawn, because algae consume oxygen overnight. The Tasset estuary has the most frequent low-oxygen events because of its shallow, slow-flushing upper reaches.
