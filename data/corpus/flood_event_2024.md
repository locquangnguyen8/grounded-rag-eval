# March 2024 Flood Event

In March 2024, 148 mm of rain fell on the Merrin catchment over three days. Salinity at all six Merrin stations dropped below 10 PSU.

The food authority closed all Merrin harvest areas. The closure lasted 19 days.

The forecasting model had flagged high closure risk for the Merrin two days before the rainfall started. After the event, the team added river-flow lag features to the model to better capture slow freshwater recovery.
