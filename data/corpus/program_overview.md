# Harbourline Estuary Monitoring Program: Overview

The Harbourline Estuary Monitoring Program (HEMP) is a fictional water-quality monitoring program used as the knowledge base for this demo. It began operating in July 2021.

HEMP monitors three estuaries: the Merrin, the Kallow and the Tasset. The network has 14 fixed monitoring stations: six on the Merrin, five on the Kallow and three on the Tasset.

The program exists to give shellfish growers and the regional food authority early warning of conditions that make harvesting unsafe, mainly low salinity after heavy rainfall and poor water quality from catchment runoff.

The program is run by a small team: one program manager, two field technicians and one data engineer. Field technicians visit every station at least once every 21 days.
