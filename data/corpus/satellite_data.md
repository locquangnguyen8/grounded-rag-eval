# Satellite Data

HEMP supplements its fixed stations with Sentinel-2 satellite imagery. Sentinel-2 provides 10 m resolution in its visible bands and revisits the region about every five days.

The program derives a turbidity proxy and a chlorophyll proxy from the imagery. Images with more than 40% cloud cover over an estuary are discarded.

Satellite data is used to understand spatial patterns between stations. It is not used directly as a closure trigger.
